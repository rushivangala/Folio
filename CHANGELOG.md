# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (in progress)

* New app in the suite: `io.github.rushivangala.folio`, built on the shared design kit, with no internet permission (the build checks it).
* Import from Photos, Files and the share sheet ("Scan with Folio"), including every page of a PDF.
* Review a multi-page scan: reorder, rotate, delete and add pages; crop by dragging corners with a magnifier; perspective flattening.
* Filters Auto, Colour, Greyscale, Black & white and Photo, with shadow and glare reduction.
* Save as PDF (Folio's own writer, A4 width, ready for a searchable text layer) or JPEG, into Documents/Folio or a chosen folder.
* Library with collections, scan details, open, share, rename and delete; scans deleted outside Folio are marked.
* Settings: filter for new pages, PDF or JPEG, save folder.
